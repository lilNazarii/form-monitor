What Sentleaf keeps,
and why.

This page describes the current private beta. Payments are not open yet. We will publish the operator’s billing details and full subscription terms before accepting live payments.

When you send a request

We save your email, any website address and message you provide, the request type, and submission time. The workspace owner can review these details and use them to respond. Requests are removed from the active request list after 90 days, or earlier when deleted by the owner. Sending a request does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Your account and monitoring

We store your account email, a salted password hash, monitor settings, schedules, check results, technical logs, and available screenshots of checked pages. Your password is not stored as readable text. These records support sign-in, running checks, explaining failures, and recovery. Removing a monitor stops its schedule; previous check reports remain stored. History access covers 7 days on Solo and the trial, or 90 days on Studio. Changing plans does not delete older records. Studio reports summarize existing check records; CSV exports contain check metadata, not Gmail message contents.

Your Gmail connection

Google handles authorization. The requested gmail.readonly permission allows reading email in the connected mailbox. Sentleaf uses it to search for the unique reference of its test message in Gmail Inbox and inspect matching messages. It does not send, delete, or mark Gmail messages as read. OAuth credentials are stored encrypted; the service needs its server key to use them.

You can disconnect Gmail in Connections. To revoke Google’s permission as well, remove Sentleaf from your Google Account’s third-party connections. Disconnecting pauses monitors that use that inbox.

Infrastructure and emails

The application and its data run on Render. Google provides the Gmail API; Resend processes password recovery and monitoring alert emails. If payments are activated later, Stripe will host payment and subscription management; Sentleaf will store customer and subscription identifiers and access status, not card numbers.

Sessions and backups

An essential, secure session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 12 hours. The current site does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. Encrypted core backups include account settings, Gmail connections, billing records, and saved requests. The server retains the latest seven copies; a separately downloaded backup can remain with the owner until deleted.

Questions, correction, or deletion

Use the contact form and select “data or deletion request”. We will review the request and verify account ownership before changing or deleting account data. Do not include passwords or private email contents. Deletion is currently handled by the owner rather than an automatic account button.
