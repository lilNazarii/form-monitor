Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Sentleaf at sentleaf.app handles your information. Sentleaf checks whether a test submission on a website form reaches the Gmail inbox you connect. The Sentleaf operator is responsible for running the service and responding to privacy requests through our contact form.

Google data use · Sharing · Protection · Retention and deletion

Google user data we access

Connecting Gmail is optional until you choose to use inbox monitoring. After you approve Google's consent screen, Sentleaf receives OAuth access and refresh tokens and your connected Gmail address. We never receive your Google password.

We request gmail.readonly. This permission technically allows reading Gmail messages and settings. Sentleaf uses it to identify your connected mailbox, search its Inbox for the unique MONITOR- reference generated for each check, and retrieve matching messages to inspect their headers and body. Retrieved messages can include other content in the same raw message. We do not browse unrelated conversations or use the permission to change mailbox settings.

How we use Google user data

We use your Gmail address to identify the inbox selected for a monitor. OAuth tokens let the server run the checks you request or schedule, including when your browser is closed.

For each check, Sentleaf submits a test message through your verified website form. It searches the connected Gmail Inbox for the test reference, then checks the matching message's subject, recipient and expected test content. The result tells you whether delivery succeeded and how long it took. This information powers your dashboard, check history, available reports and failure or recovery alerts.

Message bodies are processed in server memory during the check; Sentleaf does not save a mailbox archive or copies of message bodies. Saved check records can contain the matching Gmail message ID, recipient address, test reference, Inbox status, verification result and elapsed time. Sentleaf does not send, modify, delete or mark Gmail messages as read. Form submissions and outgoing alert emails use separate mechanisms, not Gmail sending permissions.

Sharing, transfers and disclosure of Google user data

Google user data is handled only to operate the monitoring features you choose, subject to the limited exceptions below. These service providers have the following roles:

  • Google: authenticates your Gmail connection and processes the authorized API requests used to find and verify the test email.
  • Render: hosts the Sentleaf application and storage. Google user data is processed on this infrastructure, including matching messages in server memory, stored check records and encrypted Gmail credentials and backups.
  • Resend: delivers monitoring alerts to your configured notification address. Those alerts contain your website details and the delivery result derived from the Gmail check. We do not send Gmail message bodies, Gmail message IDs or OAuth tokens to Resend. Resend also delivers account recovery emails and messages you submit through our contact form.
  • Stripe: processes payments and subscriptions using account and billing information. We do not provide Stripe with Gmail message content, Gmail check records or Google OAuth tokens.

Your own signed-in workspace displays your connected inbox and monitoring results. Other Sentleaf customers do not receive access to your connections or check records. The service operator can administer the hosting and encrypted backups for operation and recovery. We do not routinely allow people to read Google message data. Human access to specific Google user data is limited to your affirmative agreement, necessary security investigation, or a legal requirement.

We do not sell Google user data or transfer it for advertising, credit scoring or data brokerage. Other disclosures are limited to what is necessary for security or compliance with applicable law. Any transfer as part of a business sale would require your explicit prior consent. Service providers are used for their stated service functions, not for independent use of Gmail content.

How we protect sensitive data

  • Encryption in transit: the website and Google API connections use HTTPS/TLS. The website sends an HSTS header to tell supporting browsers to use HTTPS.
  • Encrypted Gmail credentials: access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with authenticated Fernet encryption. The encryption key is held in server environment configuration, separate from stored token files. Core backup archives are encrypted too.
  • Account access controls: signed-in requests are scoped to the user's workspace; backup downloads and administrative functions require the owner account. Account passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, not plaintext.
  • Session and authorization protection: secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookies protect sessions. State validation and PKCE bind Gmail authorization to the initiating session. State-changing browser requests require origin and CSRF checks.
  • Data minimization: full Gmail message bodies are not stored in check history or sent in alerts. Tokens and authorization codes are not returned in dashboard responses or Google API error messages.

These controls describe the current implementation. They do not constitute a claim that Sentleaf has already passed Google's verification or an independent security assessment.

Google API Limited Use and AI

Sentleaf's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We use Google user data only for the user-facing monitoring features described here. We do not use or transfer data obtained through Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. Sentleaf does not use Gmail data for AI model training, advertising or profiling.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Gmail message bodies are held temporarily in memory for verification. Encrypted OAuth tokens and the connected Gmail address remain in active connection storage until you disconnect that inbox or your account data is deleted. In Workspace → Connections, select Disconnect to remove the active connection and pause monitors that use it. You can also revoke Sentleaf's permission in Google Account connections. Revoking access at Google does not by itself delete historical Sentleaf records.

Account settings, monitor configuration and check records are retained to operate your account and provide history until removed through account-data deletion. The history window included in your plan controls what is visible; it is not an automatic deletion deadline for underlying records.

The server retains the latest seven encrypted core backups, replacing older copies only when a new backup succeeds. Deleted connection or account data can therefore remain in older backups until rotation. Copies separately downloaded by the operator remain until deleted; a deletion request includes review of those copies. Check history and website screenshots are not part of these core backups.

To request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated Google user data, use the contact form and choose “data or deletion request”. The operator verifies account ownership and handles deletion manually. We will explain any retention required for legal or security purposes. Do not submit passwords, OAuth tokens or private email contents.

Account, website and contact information

We store your account email, password hash, settings, verified website addresses, monitor schedules, test results, technical logs and screenshots of the monitored website. Website screenshots show the tested form, not your Gmail inbox. These records support service operation and troubleshooting.

When you contact us, we save your email, any website address and message you provide, request type and submission time. The request is shown in the owner's workspace and emailed to the owner through Resend. Contact requests are removed from the active request list after 90 days; separately delivered emails and encrypted backups have their own retention.

Paid plans renew monthly through Stripe. Sentleaf stores customer and subscription identifiers and access status, not card numbers. You can manage or cancel a paid subscription in Plan & billing.

An essential secure session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 12 hours. The current website does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts.
