GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Can I try Sentleaf now?

Yes, you can request a 7-day trial. We currently review each account and website before enabling access. Once approved, connect Gmail and start the trial from your workspace. No card is required.

Which forms are supported?

Standard Contact Form 7 forms on WordPress. The contact page must be reachable online. CAPTCHA, multi-step forms, and custom workflows are not supported yet.

What can Sentleaf read in Gmail?

Google’s read-only permission allows reading Gmail messages. Sentleaf uses it to search Inbox for its unique test references and read matching messages. It does not send, delete, or mark Gmail messages as read.

Will test emails arrive in my inbox?

Yes. Every check sends a clearly marked test message through your real form. Choose a schedule from every 6 hours, or pause it whenever you need.

Do I need to leave my computer on?

No. Checks run on our server. A local test website is an exception: its computer and public tunnel must stay online.

What happens after the free trial?

Checks pause after 7 days unless you choose an available paid plan. You are never charged automatically for starting a trial. Your saved forms stay in your workspace.
