FROM SETUP TO FIRST CHECK

One form.
A complete delivery check.

Use your existing contact page and mail settings. Form Monitor tests the route your messages already take.

01 / CONNECT

Add your contact page

Enter the page URL and confirm the form’s field names. Standard Contact Form 7 fields are filled in for you.

02 / CHECK

Run a real submission

The monitor sends a clearly marked test message and looks for its unique reference in your connected Gmail Inbox.

03 / MONITOR

Choose a check interval

See each result in your workspace. A failed check is retried once before the cycle is marked as failed.

IN THIS VERSION

The evidence behind
every result.

Inbox confirmation

A success requires the matching message in Gmail Inbox. A copy in Sent or Spam does not count.

Problem & recovery alerts

Resend sends an alert after a failed cycle, then another when the monitor recovers.

A history you can inspect

Review attempts, reported errors and available page screenshots. Keep the evidence when you remove a monitor.

Schedules you control

Run checks manually or choose an interval for each form. Pause checks whenever you need to.

LOCAL PREVIEW

Start with your own website.

This version runs on your computer with a selected Gmail account for each monitor. Keep your computer, website and workspace running for scheduled checks.

Standard Contact Form 7 forms are supported. Forms with CAPTCHA or custom multi-step flows need a separate integration.
