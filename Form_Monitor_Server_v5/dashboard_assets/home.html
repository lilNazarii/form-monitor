04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
up front.

A focused tool, with a clear scope.

What kinds of forms can I monitor?+

This version supports standard Contact Form 7 forms on WordPress. CAPTCHA, multi-step forms, and custom workflows need a separate integration. The form page must be publicly reachable and enabled for this beta.

Do I need to keep my computer on?+

No. Sentleaf runs on a server and keeps checking when your browser is closed. If the website you’re testing runs locally on your computer, that computer and its public tunnel still need to stay on.

What access does Sentleaf have to Gmail?+

You connect through Google with read-only Gmail permission. That permission allows reading email; Sentleaf uses it to find and read matching test messages in Inbox. It does not send, delete, or mark Gmail messages as read.

Will test messages appear in my inbox?+

Yes. Checks follow the form’s real mail route, so clearly marked test messages arrive in its configured Gmail inbox. You control how often checks run and can pause them whenever needed.

Can I start using Sentleaf today?+

Sentleaf is in a private beta. Workspace access and monitored websites are enabled individually. Use the contact page to request early access. If your account is already enabled, sign in and connect your first form. The pricing section shows payment availability. Connecting Gmail and enabling a website are required before monitoring starts.
